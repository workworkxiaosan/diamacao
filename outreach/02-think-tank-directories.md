# 國際智庫名錄登記部署包

> 限制：兩個名錄均為人工編輯/提名制，無法代為自動註冊。以下為現成提交文本與通道，複製即可提交。

## A. On Think Tanks（優先，接受自主提交）

- 網站：https://onthinktanks.org
- 通道：網站底部 Contact / 「Submit your organisation」入口，或郵件至其編輯部（以官網當前公佈的聯繫方式為準）。
- 定位建議：DIA 屬「政策導向行業協會／智庫型組織」（policy-oriented association / think tank-type organisation），如實填寫，勿標榜為純研究型智庫。

### 提交文本（英文，直接複製）

```
Name: Macao Digital Innovation Association (DIA)
Native name: 澳門數字創新協會 / Associação de Inovação Digital de Macau
Type: Non-profit, policy-oriented association (think tank-type organisation)
Country/Region: Macao SAR, China
Founded: 2026
Website: https://www.diamacao.com (available in Chinese, English, Portuguese, French and Spanish)
Contact: info@diamacao.com

Profile (120 words):
The Macao Digital Innovation Association (DIA) is a non-profit association based in Macao SAR, China. It promotes the integration of digital technologies with Macao's diversifying industries and operates a one-stop Go-Global Support Program for enterprises entering international markets. DIA's work spans digital economy policy monitoring, cross-border matchmaking, and China–Portuguese-speaking Countries cooperation, with a particular focus on Portuguese-, Spanish- and French-speaking markets and Belt and Road partner countries. Its activities include policy briefings on China's outward investment and digital trade rules, enterprise matchmaking events (including at the Macao International Trade and Investment Fair), and Beijing–Macao science and technology cooperation exchanges co-organised with Zhongguancun Science City.
```

## B. 賓大 Global Go To Think Tank Index（UPenn TTCSP）

- 網站：https://thinktanks.fpri.upenn.edu
- 機制：**專家提名制**，每年開放提名窗口（通常年中至下半年），不接受純自助登記。
- 務實路徑：
  1. 先完成 On Think Tanks 登記與 Wikidata 建檔，累積公開研究產出（政策簡報/研究報告）。
  2. 邀請已入索引的友好智庫（如合作高校研究機構）在下一提名季提名 DIA。
  3. 提名材料直接用上方英文 Profile，另附年度活動清單（可引用官網 news/policy 頁）。
- 提醒：DIA 成立於 2026 年，建議 2027 提名季再正式參與，當年重點放在研究產出與國際能見度。
